# Reference Counting Protocol — v1.0

Status: **v1.0, 2026-11-23.** Freeze this file (git tag `counting-protocol-v1.0`) before the first count is entered.
Any change after that becomes v1.1 and is recorded in the change log at the bottom. Every saved count stores the
protocol version it was made under.

## 1. Purpose

Reference counts are the **independent oracle** for counting accuracy: KPI-1, KPI-4, KPI-5, and the counting rows
of AC-1 and AC-3. They must be produced by people, independently of the pipeline. A count produced or suggested by
any model is never a reference count.

## 2. Unit of counting

- **One clip** (one Barbados one-minute file, or one user-supplied video) × **one counting line** × **one
  direction** × **one vehicle class** = one cell.
- Counting lines are the ones in `configs/cameras/<camera>.yaml`. The counting tool draws them on the video. They
  are the same lines the pipeline uses, so the two counts measure the same thing.

## 3. What counts as a crossing

1. A vehicle is counted when the **middle of its front or rear wheels, where they touch the road**, passes the
   drawn line. When the wheels are hidden, use the bottom edge of the vehicle body.
2. Each vehicle is counted **once per line**, even if it stops on the line, reverses or is hidden for a moment.
3. Direction is the side of the line it ends up on, using the direction names the tool shows (for example
   "entering" means moving down the image across the approach line on camera 1).
4. Vehicles that are crossing when the clip starts are counted **only if their contact point is still before the
   line in the first frame**. Vehicles that have not finished crossing when the clip ends are **not** counted.
5. Parked vehicles and objects that never cross are not counted.
6. Segments of the line outside the drawn ends do not count.

## 4. Vehicle classes

| Class | Includes | Excludes |
|-------|----------|----------|
| car | cars, SUVs, pickups, taxis, small vans that seat ≤ 9 | minibuses carrying passengers |
| bus | full-size buses, minibuses / route taxis (ZR vans in Barbados), coaches | |
| truck | lorries, delivery trucks, box vans used for goods, tankers, tractors | pickups |
| motorcycle | motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, motorised tricycles | bicycles |
| bicycle | pedal bicycles | |
| other | anything that does not fit, or you cannot classify after replaying | |

If you are unsure between two classes, pick the more likely one and tick **"uncertain"** for that clip with a note.
Never leave a vehicle out because its class is unclear: use **other**.

## 5. Independence and blindness

- Each clip is counted by **two annotators working independently**. They do not see each other's counts until both
  have saved.
- Annotators **must not look at pipeline outputs** (annotated videos, the Counting page, events.csv) for a clip
  before saving their count. The counting tool shows only the raw clip and the lines.
- Use the video controls freely: pause, rewind, slow-motion scrubbing with the frame stepper. Record the time spent.

## 6. Adjudication

- When both annotators agree on every cell of a clip, that clip's reference is final.
- When they disagree on any cell, a third person (or the two together) recounts **only the disagreeing cells** and
  saves an adjudicated record with a short reason. Until then, those cells are excluded from accuracy figures and
  listed as "awaiting adjudication".
- Agreement statistics (exact agreement, mean absolute difference, ICC) are reported **before** adjudication.

## 7. Sampling plan

`data/annotations/reference_counts/plan_v1.csv` lists the clips to count. It was generated by
`scripts/make_counting_plan.py` (seeded and deterministic) before anyone counted:

- Barbados camera 1, two 23-minute windows (21 Oct 02:00 night, 09:00 morning peak): 8 clips each, spread across the
  window.
- The user-supplied expressway sample (20.7 s): 1 clip.

These clips are **development evidence**: they measure O2 and tune nothing. AC-3 acceptance clips must come from
cameras or days not used in development (planned: cameras 3–4 or a different day), drawn later under the same
protocol.

## 8. Records

Each save writes `data/annotations/reference_counts/<annotator>/<clip_id>.json` containing:
- clip ID, the clip's SHA-256 and the geometry file's SHA-256 (checked on save),
- annotator ID (initials or roll number, no names), role (`primary`, `secondary`, `adjudicator`),
- protocol version, start and finish times, the counts per cell, an "uncertain" flag and notes.

These files are small and **are committed to git**: they are the project's reference evidence.
