Models in this prototype

Detector: RT-DETR (transformer)Replaces YOLOv8. Boxes and classes are simulated here.
Tracker: DeepSORTReplaces ByteTrack. IDs shown on each box; one count per ID.
Forecaster: ARIMA-style AR(2)Replaces LSTM. Fitted live in your browser by least squares on interval counts.
Congestion rulePredicted count vs road capacity: below 40% low, to 75% medium, above high.
